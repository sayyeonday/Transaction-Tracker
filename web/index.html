Expense Tracker

Turn your CIBC or BMO CSV exports into a tidy Excel workbook you can categorize and chart.

100% private. Everything runs inside your browser. Your transactions are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere — when you close this tab, nothing remains.

One thing stays with you: the finance.xlsx you download is unencrypted and holds your financial details. Keep it somewhere safe and out of shared or auto-synced folders — once it’s on your device, protecting it is your responsibility.
