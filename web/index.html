Expense Tracker

Turn your CIBC or BMO CSV exports into a tidy Excel workbook you can categorize and chart.

100% private. Everything runs inside your browser. Your transactions are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere — when you close this tab, nothing remains. One thing stays with you: the finance.xlsx you download is unencrypted and holds your financial details. Keep it somewhere safe and out of shared or auto-synced folders — once it’s on your device, protecting it is your responsibility.

1. Credit card exports

Your CIBC or BMO credit-card CSV file(s). You can select more than one.

2. Debit / chequing exports

Your CIBC or BMO debit / chequing CSV file(s).

3. Previous workbook — optional

Already categorized a workbook before? Add it here and your category choices are kept.

  1. Pick your CSV files above and click Build my workbook.
  2. Open the downloaded finance.xlsx in Excel.
  3. On the Merchants and Names tabs, choose a category from the dropdowns.
  4. The Overview and Monthly tabs update automatically — no internet needed.
